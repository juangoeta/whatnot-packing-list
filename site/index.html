What you get

One page, every order named

Built from your show recording and the order list Whatnot actually holds. Opened on a phone at the packing bench, on a laptop, or printed. These are real pages from a real show, with buyer names and order numbers replaced.

Pictures and the mic, on the orders worth a look

Most orders come back as one line of text. The ones where the camera and the mic did not fully agree are marked Needs your check, each with the reason, three sharp frames from the recording around the moment of sale, and what was said on the mic with the buyer's name and the item number highlighted. You glance at a handful instead of rewatching the show.

Corrected for the receipt offset. Every stream runs ahead of or behind the receipts by a different amount. The list measures that from your own callouts, so the frames show the sale, not the item before it.

Save evidence card. One picture per order with the frames, the words and the order data, for the day a buyer says the wrong thing arrived.

Packing slips with the product on them

One slip per shipping label, in the same order as Whatnot's label export, so the slips come off the printer in the pile order of the labels. The product is written out: brand, item, shade or size, bundle count. Your buyer sees what they bought, not "random".

Split shipments handled. A buyer with two labels gets "label 1 of 2" and "2 of 2", each with its own tracking number, so the right label goes on the right box. The unit is Whatnot's shipment, not the username.

Works at the bench

Tick boxes on every order. The page remembers what you packed on that phone or computer, so you can stop and pick up later.

Search and filter by buyer, item number, product or tracking. Hide packed, needs-check only, hide giveaways, flag reason.

Resend after you pack. Click Send again and the list opens with a "Changed since your last list" box: new orders in green, removed orders in red with "pull this out of the buyer's box if already packed", moved orders with the old and new package, tracking numbers added. Failed payments and late cancellations stop being a surprise.

Sales the orders do not show. A sale heard on the mic with no order in Whatnot (the payment never went through) is listed separately so you do not pack it.

Install

Your first show in three steps

Chrome on a computer (the Whatnot app does not have the Shipments tools). Five minutes the first time, one click after that.

Install the extension

Add Whatnot Packing List Sender from the Chrome Web Store.

Temporary, until the Web Store listing is live: unpacked zip
  1. Download the extension zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped extension folder.

Paste your code in Settings

The welcome page opens on install. Paste the access code from your email and click Save. That is the whole setup.

Send the show

Open whatnot.com/dashboard/shipments, pick the show in the first dropdown, keep the status on Unfulfilled, and click Send show for packing list. Leave the tab open until the card says "Sent". It tells you how many shows your code has left.

The card on the Shipments page. It refuses to send a short list: it counts the orders the packages should contain and waits until they are all on the page. Sample page with placeholder buyers.

FAQ

Questions sellers ask

What does it cost?

{{PRICE_PER_SHOW}} for one show, or {{PRICE_MONTHLY}} a month for {{SHOWS_PER_MONTH}} shows. A show is a show, whether it had 40 orders or 900. Resending the same show after you pack (to catch cancellations and new tracking numbers) costs nothing.

What leaves my computer?

Two things, only when you click Send: the link to the show recording that your own Shipments page already holds, and the order list shown on that page (buyer username, order number, listing title, price, package, receipt time, tracking number and carrier).

No video is downloaded to your computer. The service fetches the recording from Whatnot's link on its own machine. The extension has no login, no password, cannot read other pages, and can contact only whatnot.com and the Who Got What service.

The recording and the order list are kept while your list is being prepared and used for nothing else. Full text: privacy policy.

How long does it take?

Sending takes under a minute on your side. The list comes back in {{TURNAROUND_SMALL}} for shows up to about 150 orders and {{TURNAROUND_LARGE}} for bigger shows. Send right after you buy labels so the tracking numbers are on the slips; a resend later refreshes them.

What if an order on the list is wrong?

Every order the camera and the mic did not fully agree on is already marked Needs your check with the reason, three pictures and the words, so you can decide in seconds before sealing. Giveaways are marked too, because the item on camera at the draw is often not the prize.

If something slipped through, reply to your code email or write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the order number. We look at the recording again and send a corrected list. The list also says how it was checked: the measured receipt offset, how many orders matched a callout, how many were matched by time only.

Sellers say "never rely on video receipts for packing". Isn't this that?

They are right about the receipts: the clip starts about 30 seconds early, a failed payment makes the order vanish, a late payment shifts everything, and clicking through 300 clips one at a time is a full day. That advice is about eyeballing single clips with no cross-check.

This is the opposite of that. The list starts from the orders Whatnot actually holds, not from the video. It measures the offset between the stream and the receipts from your own callouts instead of trusting the clip start. It reads the words you said at each sale (the buyer's name, the item number, "bundle of three", "the blush one") and matches them to the frames. Sales heard on the mic with no order behind them are listed separately so you do not pack them. And it tells you, order by order, which ones it is sure of and which ones you should look at.

Keep tagging, binning or logging during the show if that is your system. This is the record at the end that says what each buyer was promised.

What kind of shows does it work for?

Any show where the listing title does not describe the product: random pulls, numbered singles ("#1 to #200"), mystery bundles, buyer's choice ("vanilla or blush?"), liquidation lots. Beauty, sunglasses and eyewear, cards, clothing, golf balls, snacks have all been through it. It needs you to say the winner's name or the item number on the mic, which most sellers already do. Shows in English for now.

Does it change anything in my Whatnot account?

No. It only reads the Shipments page you are already looking at. It cannot buy labels, cancel orders, message buyers or touch listings. Not affiliated with Whatnot.
