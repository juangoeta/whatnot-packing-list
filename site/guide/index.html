1. Install the extension

Chrome on a computer. The Whatnot app has no Shipments tools, so the phone cannot do this part.

  1. Open Whatnot Packing List Sender on the Chrome Web Store and click Add to Chrome.
  2. Pin it: click the puzzle icon next to the address bar, then the pin next to the extension.
Temporary, until the Web Store listing is live: unpacked zip
  1. Download the extension zip and unzip it. You get a folder named extension.
  2. Go to chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode at the top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the extension folder. Leave the folder where it is; Chrome reads it from there.

2. Paste the address and the code

  1. Click the extension icon. A small Settings page opens.
  2. Paste the service address from your email into the first box (it ends in /show).
  3. Paste your access code into the second box and click Save.
  4. Chrome asks whether the extension may contact that address. Choose Allow. That is the only address it can talk to besides whatnot.com.

The access code and the address come in the same email as your receipt, within {{CODE_WITHIN}} hours of paying. Lost it? Write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

3. Send the show

  1. Buy your shipping labels first, so the tracking numbers end up on the slips.
  2. Go to whatnot.com/dashboard/shipments. In the first dropdown (All Shows & Marketplace) pick the show.
  3. Keep the status filter on Unfulfilled. The card warns you if the page shows all orders.
  4. Click Send show for packing list in the card at the bottom right. Leave the tab open until the card says Sent, with the order and package counts and how many show credits you have left. About 25 seconds for a 340-order show.

If the show has more than 50 packages the card switches the page to "Show 250" itself and continues; shows over 250 packages are read page by page. If the card says the orders could not be read, reload the page and click again.

Done. Your list arrives by email: {{TURNAROUND_SMALL}} for shows up to about 150 orders, {{TURNAROUND_LARGE}} for bigger ones.

Reading the list

The big pink number
The item number from the listing title ("Beauty pulls #42" is item 42), the number you stickered on the product. If the show used more than one listing name, it is written in small letters under the number.
What to pack
What was on camera when the countdown hit zero, checked against what you said on the mic: brand, product, shade or size, bundle count.
Each package is one label
A buyer with two labels shows "label 1 of 2" and "2 of 2", each with its own tracking number. Pack by label, not by username.
Yellow rows
Need your check before sealing. The reason is on the row. Show evidence opens three pictures from the recording and what was said on the mic, with the buyer's name and the item number in bold.
Sold at
The clock time, and under it in grey the position in the Whatnot replay (h:mm:ss). Drag the replay scrubber there to watch the sale.
Tick boxes
Check items off as you pack. The page remembers on that phone or computer; a different device starts empty.
Order numbers
Hidden by default to keep the table readable. The arrows next to the dimmed "Order" column bring them back.
Search and filter
Any item number, buyer, product word or tracking number. Chips for hide packed, needs check only, hide giveaways, and one per flag reason.

Giveaways are marked too. The box on camera at the draw is often not the prize, so the row says "giveaway prize" and leaves the item to you.

Heard on the mic, no order in Whatnot. A separate box lists sales you called out that have no order behind them: the payment did not go through or it was cancelled during the show. The item is probably still in your pile. Do not pack it.

Packing slips

Click Print slips at the top of the list. One slip per shipping label, in the same order as Whatnot's label export, so the slips come off the printer in the pile order of the labels. Paper: 4 x 6 label, or letter with two slips per sheet.

Resend after you pack

Cancellations and failed payments arrive after you have packed, and tracking numbers appear when you buy more labels. Click Send show for packing list again on the same show. Resending is free and the list is rebuilt in place.

  • The page opens with a "Changed since your last list" box: new orders in green with the product, removed orders in red with "pull this out of the buyer's box if already packed", moved orders with the old and new package, and the count of tracking numbers added.
  • Your tick boxes survive. Orders keep their place.
  • The CSV gets a Change column (NEW / MOVED, and the REMOVED rows at the end).

Checking a flagged order in Whatnot yourself

The evidence panel usually settles it. If you want to see the moment in Whatnot's own video receipt:

  1. On the Shipments page, click Expand on the order, then Video Receipt. The clip starts playing.
  2. Expect the clip to start about 30 seconds before the sale, and sometimes to be off by more. The item on screen at the very start is usually the previous one.
  3. Better: take the grey replay position from the list's "Sold at" column and drag the full replay there. That time is corrected for the stream's offset.

Receipts are kept by Whatnot for 60 days and vanish when a payment fails. The list keeps its own frames and words, so the evidence cards stay available after that.
