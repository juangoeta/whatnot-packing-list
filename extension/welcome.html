Your show, rewatched for you.

After a live, one click on your Shipments page. Back comes a packing list that names the real product in every order, with pictures from the moment of the sale and a short list of orders worth a second look.

No login, no password. The extension reads your own Shipments page and sends two things: the show link and the order list. No video touches your computer.

How it works
  1. Enter your access code

    It is in your welcome email. Paste it in the form at the bottom of this page and click Save.

  2. Open Seller Hub › Shipments

    Pick the show at the top of the page and keep the status on Unfulfilled. Buy your labels as usual.

  3. Click Send

    Send show for packing list
    The card sits at the bottom right of the Shipments page. About 25 seconds for a 340-order show.

  4. Get the link

    The packing list and slips arrive by email, usually the same day. Open the link on a phone or a laptop; it works offline once loaded.
