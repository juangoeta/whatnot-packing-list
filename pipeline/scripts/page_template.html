    How to read this list

    • The big pink number is the item number from the listing title ("Beauty pulls #42" is item 42). If the show used more than one listing name, the name appears in small letters under the number so two #42s are not mixed up.
    • Order numbers are hidden. Click the arrows next to the dimmed "Order" column name to show them when a label is in doubt. The arrows next to any column name fold it away or bring it back.
    • The small grey time under "Sold at" is the position in the Whatnot replay (h:mm:ss from the start of the recording). Drag the replay scrubber there to watch the sale.
    • "What to pack" is what was on camera when the countdown hit zero, checked against what you said on the mic: brand, shade, bundle size.
    • Each package is one shipping label. A buyer with two labels shows "label 1 of 2" and "2 of 2" with their own tracking numbers.
    • Yellow rows need your check before sealing. "Show evidence" opens three pictures from the recording and what was said on the mic.
    • Put a check mark in the little white box as you pack. It stays checked when you close and reopen this page on the same phone or computer; a different device starts with the boxes empty.

          How this list was checked

            Whatnot's video receipts do not always show the right product: the clip starts about 30 seconds before the sale and the recording runs behind or ahead of real time by a different amount on every stream, sometimes drifting during the show. This list corrects for that by measuring the offset from your own callouts, cross-checking every order against the pictures and what was said on the mic, and reconciling with the orders Whatnot actually holds. Those checks catch most mistakes, but a 100% match cannot be guaranteed, so the rows marked for a double-check deserve a look before sealing.
